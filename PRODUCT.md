# Product: Prophet

Prophet is a durable forecasting agent for R&D. A user states a goal (for example, "forecast weekly demand for twelve weeks"). A planner chooses steps: it calls TimesFM 3 for a forecast, runs sandboxed analytics, asks the user a structured question when a decision is needed, and produces a chart, spreadsheet, report and PDF. Missions survive crashes and resume from the last recorded step.

- Surface: the console. A missions list, a chat-style transcript of each mission, question cards the user answers, and an artefact panel with the files and their sha256 hashes.
- Mode: Operate. The visitor completes a task. Scanability, precision and native web conventions outrank expression. The brand lives in the details.
- Users: analysts and planners who review a forecast, answer the agent's questions and download the pack. They work at a desk, often late, in a dim room, and sometimes on a phone.
- Job to be done: start a forecast, follow what the agent did and why, answer the questions it asks, and take the files away.

## Brand commitments (binding)

- Identity: Dune-meets-space, as the user asked. A dark desert-night base, sand for text and quiet structure, ember for action and attention. No Dune names, logos, quotations or trademarked motifs.
- Type: a serif display voice for headings and the wordmark; a clean sans for interface and body text; a monospace only for hashes, identifiers and JSON, where the content really is data.
- Research use only. The forecast engine uses non-commercial TimesFM 3 weights, so every forecast surface carries that note.
- No invented claims: no prices, customers, benchmarks or capabilities the product does not have. Demonstration numbers are labelled synthetic.
- Accessibility: body text at 4.5:1 or better, visible focus rings in ember, every control reachable by keyboard, and no horizontal scroll at 375 px.
