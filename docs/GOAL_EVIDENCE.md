## Correction and freshness investigation (20 Nov 2026)
Correction to the audit entry above: the "recent_precision 25%" figure is a window-edge artefact, not a defect the agent shows on its own. `recent_precision` is the last 20 flags in replay order, which covers only the last two or three days. Those alerts were scored against expiries cut at the window end, so they were marked false even when right.
- Scorer fix (commit on this date, `sim/scorer.py`): freshness flags use expiries to the data end (6 Nov 2026 on the twin); an alert is scored only when its lots' use-by dates fall inside the data (`immature_excluded` reports the count). Test: `tests/test_scorer_maturity.py`.
- Replay, freshness, 1 Jun to 23 Oct 2026 (Synthetic replay): 1,159 alerts, 86.6% useful, recall 56.6%, dollar recall 87.6%, 0 immature excluded (the window ends 14 days before the data end and the horizon is 21 days, so the 6 Nov end covers all alerts; check when the default window changes). Monthly precision: Jun 87.5%, Jul 90.7%, Aug 89.5%, Sep 82.1%, Oct 82.0%.
- The September and October dip (about 6 to 7 points, roughly 3 standard errors, with autocorrelation so treat as indicative) is not caused by the scorer. Planted product shocks (demand surges and promotions) overlap only 20 of 1,159 alerts, and the dip persists without them.
- Mechanism found: actual sell-through of each lot ran above the forecast sell-through from September. Ratio of actual to forecast sell-through: all categories 1.17 (Sep) and 1.16 (Oct), against 1.14, 1.06, 1.00 (Jun to Aug). Seafood 1.15 to 1.19 in Sep and Oct. Forecast at-risk quantities were overstated, so alerts were false. Per-lot median of forecast at-risk to actual expired is 1.00 in every month, so the expiry maths is calibrated on average; the bias comes from the demand level.
- Consistent with the backtest bias (TimesFM under-forecasts by about 5% on the twin). The mechanism is demand rising faster than the forecast tracks it; this is not yet tested for causality.
- Open: whether a bias correction (recent-error scaling) fixes it without hurting the Jun to Aug calibration. Needs a decision; see backlog B16.
