## Verified in this session

- `seasonal_year` raises `IndexError` when the horizon exceeds the season (200 points, horizon 60, season 52).
- `grounding._observed` yields a sub-agent's written answer as evidence, so its unsourced figures pass the lead's check.
- `goal._calls_used` counts top-level tool calls only; sub-agent calls are missing from the planner's count.
- `evaluator.v8.md` tells the model not to add licence or research-use wording. `CLAUDE.md` says TimesFM 3 outputs are
  research use only. The owner then locked a permanent rule (2026-11-22): licence wording lives only in Markdown
  documents, never in the app. See GOAL.md and ARCHITECTURE.md §5.

## Our own errors found by the audits

- The scaled-figure match from round 14 is loose at coarse precision: "1.2m" matches any value within 50,000 of 1.2
  million. This is inherent to rounding a figure to 1.2m, and the wider 1% approximation tolerance was already there.
  Recorded as a known limit; a figure written to two significant digits cannot be checked more tightly.
- The total-range text said "the truth lies between" the two bounds. That is false in general: the independent bound is
  not a limit, because negatively correlated days give a narrower total. Reworded as the range under two assumptions.
